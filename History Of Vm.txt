Ek Interview-Style Answer
Agar sir puchhe:

"OpenStack aur KVM me kya difference hai?"

Answer:
KVM ek hypervisor hai jo actual virtual machines run karta hai, jabki OpenStack ek cloud management platform hai jo KVM hypervisors, networking, storage aur virtual machines ko centrally manage karta hai.

"ESXi aur vCenter me kya difference hai?"

Answer:
ESXi actual hypervisor hai jo VMs run karta hai. vCenter management server hai jo multiple ESXi hosts ko centrally manage karta hai.

"VMware aur Hyper-V me difference?"

Answer:
VMware ka hypervisor ESXi hai, jabki Hyper-V Microsoft ka virtualization platform hai. Dono virtual machines run karte hain, lekin alag vendors aur ecosystems ke hain.
